hi guys!! miss you already; excited to have you here!! here's some recco's from me:

Food:

  • Pho 37 Richmond: great food, awesome plaza for the crowdpleaser mango sago after at Alfa tea. I personally go across the street to hanlin tea for mango drink with rice mochi

  • alternatively, bun cha ca hoang yen for best of the best Viet in Vancouver

  • finding Xian: north Chinese food, think cold sesame noodle and chili oil noodle. Nice for an easy lunch. Ahzam get the tomato egg chili noodle it slaps. Alternatively go Old Xian and get chili oil noodles and a big plate of cumin lamb

  • Zab bite: best Thai I've had outside of Thailand. Pad see ew with the actual thai noodles instead of hor fun

  • Osteria Elio Volpe: incredible italian spot

  • vintage and provisions: """secret'''" fancy late night cocktail bar

  • nemesis coffee: voted one of the best coffee shops in the world for really good reason. Pastries to physically die for

  • Costco food court: glizzy eating competition

  • lunch lady: michelin bib gourmand viet place. i think i have a bias for viet food guys

other coffee, matcha, bakery

  • matcha: cultivate, whisk matcha/level V bakery
  • cowdog suuuper trendy matcha place
  • Oidé Coffee #2 coffee experience to nemesis
